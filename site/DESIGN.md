# Design System: LEXO

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Copy-Shop Scan Bed"**

The site is a scanner bed. Pages lie on a cool gray glass platen under corner registration marks, and a single orange scan lamp is the only thing that glows. Everything else is copy paper and toner: a bright, cool, slightly blue-white ground, near-black ink in three strengths, hairline rules, and a mono folio address strip that tells you where you are in the document the way a printed page's running head does. Dark theme is the lid closed: toner ground, paper-white type, the same orange.

Density is editorial rather than dashboard. Sections are long, ruled off with one hairline, and carry their content in ledgers, ruled lists, and definition rows instead of cards. Real product imagery (the PySide6 window, the walkthrough videos) is the only illustration, and it is always laid on the platen. The type does the rest: Archivo's width axis stretches headlines wide and heavy, Martian Mono condensed to 87% speaks for addresses, commands, and labels, and Noto Sans Myanmar carries Burmese at its own, looser rhythm.

English and Burmese are both first-class. Burmese is never a translated afterthought set in the English ramp: it has its own display sizes, its own line heights, zero tracking, and a build step that restricts line breaks to the spaces the copy was written with.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cool copy-paper ground, toner ink, xerox-gray platen; no cream, no gradients.
- One accent, the logo's scan orange, spent only on scanning, the current/active state, and the primary path.
- Proof red exists for corrections and errors and nothing else.
- Every screenshot and video sits on a platen with corner registration marks.
- Squared, machine-key buttons that press down on click.
- Mono folio strip under the masthead on every page.
- Motion is one lamp sweep, animated on transform and clip-path only.

## Colors

A cool, near-neutral paper-and-toner palette with one rationed signal orange and one reserved correction red.

### Primary
- **Scan Lamp Orange** ({colors.scan}): the logo orange. The scan lamp bar and its grip, the 2px underline on the current nav item, the left border of the current docs link, prose link underlines, blockquote rules, the text caret, and the hover tint mixed into the ink button. In dark theme it shifts barely warmer ({colors.scan-dark}).
- **Scan Ink** ({colors.scan-ink}): the orange darkened for text-weight use: focus outlines, the current workflow step number, the "copied" state, icon accents on hover. Dark theme lightens it ({colors.scan-ink-dark}) for contrast.
- **Scan Wash** ({colors.scan-wash}): text selection only.

### Secondary
- **Proof Red** ({colors.proof}; dark {colors.proof-dark}): a proofreader's mark. Strike-through color on the wrong reading and the color of the inserted correction in the workflow's proof slip, and errors. Nowhere decorative.

### Neutral
- **Copy Paper** ({colors.paper}): page ground. Cool, not cream.
- **Raised Sheet** ({colors.paper-raised}): a sheet lifted off the paper: the install ticket, copy buttons, the selected install tab.
- **Platen Glass** ({colors.platen}): the scanner bed behind imagery, command lines, code blocks, inline code, setup notes, tab tracks, hover fills on header tools.
- **Platen Deep** ({colors.platen-deep}): the darker step of the glass.
- **Toner Ink** ({colors.ink}): headings, primary text, the ink button, 1.5px heavy rules that open ledgers and lists.
- **Ink 2** ({colors.ink-2}): body copy, ledes, nav links at rest.
- **Ink 3** ({colors.ink-3}): folios, captions, prompts, registration marks, dimmed workflow steps.
- **Hairline Rule** ({colors.rule}) and **Strong Rule** ({colors.rule-strong}): section dividers and row borders; button-line and tag outlines, link underlines at rest.
- **Screen Ground** ({colors.screen-ground}): the dark fill behind a screenshot or video while it loads, matching the app window.

Dark theme ("lid closed") swaps every neutral to its `-dark` token; the roles do not change. The home page's closing lid band uses the dark values in both themes.

The export excerpt's `mask-image` fade uses {colors.ink} as its opaque stop; a mask reads only alpha, so no new colour is introduced.

### Named Rules
**The Rationed Lamp Rule.** Orange marks only three things: the scan itself, the current/active state, and the primary path. If an element is orange and is none of those, it is wrong.

**The Proof Mark Rule.** Proof red appears only where something is corrected or has failed. It never decorates, highlights, or signals importance.

**The Cool Paper Rule.** The ground is cool copy paper ({colors.paper}), not cream, beige, or warm off-white.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Variable, width axis 104-118% (with "Archivo Display Fallback": local Arial Bold at size-adjust 115%)
**Body Font:** Archivo Variable at 100% width (with "Archivo Fallback": local Arial at size-adjust 98%)
**Label/Mono Font:** Martian Mono Variable at 87% width (with Consolas at 117.8%, then Menlo/DejaVu Sans Mono/Courier New at 107.6%)
**Burmese Font:** Padauk (with "Padauk Fallback": Myanmar Text / Myanmar Sangam MN / Noto Sans Myanmar)

**Character:** a wide, heavy grotesk headline over a plain reading face, with a condensed mono acting as the machine's own voice (addresses, commands, counters). Every web face has a measured, size-adjusted local fallback so the swap barely reflows lines.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (790, wdth 112, clamp 2.5rem to 5.5rem, line-height 0.96, -0.034em, balanced): page H1s.
- **Headline** (740, wdth 108, clamp 1.85rem to 3.1rem, 1.02, -0.026em, balanced): section H2s.
- **Title** (660, wdth 104, 1.25rem, 1.25, -0.012em): H3s, walkthrough titles, list terms. Prose H2 is the xl step at 720/wdth 108; prose H3 the md step at 660.
- **Lede** (400, clamp 1.125rem to 1.35rem, 1.5, Ink 2, max 38em, pretty wrap): the paragraph under every headline.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): running text; prose column capped at 44rem.
- **Label / Folio** (Martian Mono wdth 87, label step, +0.06em, uppercase, Ink 3): folio strip, breadcrumbs, docs group labels, dates, video lengths. Tags use the label step at +0.05em inside a 1px outline.
- **Code** (Martian Mono wdth 87, 0.86em): inline code, commands (ui step, -0.01em), code blocks (caption step, line-height 1.7).

### Small-Text Scale
Every size at or below the reading face, and the small display steps just above it, comes from one named scale defined on `:root` as `--fs-*` (frontmatter `typography.fs-*`):
- **micro** (0.68rem): the smallest annotations, such as codepoint labels.
- **label** (0.72rem): folios, tags, export file names.
- **xs** (0.8rem): copy buttons, install tabs, workflow step numbers, the Burmese parts of folios and tags.
- **caption** (0.86rem): figure and scan-compare captions, install notes, prose code blocks.
- **ui** (0.92rem): commands, docs sidebar links, header tools.
- **sm** (0.95rem): primary nav links, prose tables, footer links.
- **base** (1.0625rem): body and prose.
- **md** (1.2rem): prose H3, Burmese H3, mobile nav rows.
- **lg** (1.35rem): the wordmark, Burmese prose H2, proof-slip text.
- **xl** (1.6rem): prose H2.

### Component Display Sizes
A few headings deliberately sit outside the main display/headline/title ramp. They keep their ramp's weight, width, and tracking and change only the size:
- **Home hero title** (clamp(2.5rem, 1.2rem + 4.4vw, 4.6rem); Burmese clamp(1.9rem, 1.1rem + 2.6vw, 3.3rem), each line kept whole): a narrower display so the hero copy fits its column.
- **Article title** (clamp(2rem, 1.3rem + 2.8vw, 3.4rem)): blog post H1s in the headline voice.
- **Feature section title** (clamp(1.6rem, 1.2rem + 1.4vw, 2.4rem)): feature-page section headings.
- **Workflow step title** (clamp(1.3rem, 1.1rem + 0.8vw, 1.75rem)): a step on the scan bed.
- **"Who it's for" list items** (620, wdth 106, clamp(1.15rem, 1rem + 0.6vw, 1.5rem)): ruled link rows on the home page.
- **Glyph specimen word** (Myanmar face 600, clamp(3.5rem, 2.5rem + 4vw, 6rem), line-height 1.5): the one large Burmese specimen.
- **Burmese lede** (clamp(1.05rem, 0.98rem + 0.3vw, 1.2rem), line-height 1.95).

**The Named Step Rule.** New small text takes a `--fs-*` step; a literal rem size below the reading face is a bug.

### Burmese
- Body and ledes run at line-height 1.95; captions 1.9; Burmese H3 1.7, H2 1.5, display 1.42.
- Burmese has its own smaller display ramp: display clamp(2rem, 1.2rem + 3.2vw, 3.9rem) at 700, headline clamp(1.55rem, 1.1rem + 1.7vw, 2.35rem) at 700, title 1.2rem (md step).
- Letter-spacing is always 0 and uppercase transforms are dropped; a folio's Burmese parts switch to the Myanmar face at the xs step while its Latin parts (`LEXO`, version, licence) keep the mono.
- Lines break only at spaces and zero-width spaces. A post-build integration wraps each Myanmar run between them in a `white-space: nowrap` span, because the browser's segmenter splits words mid-syllable. `overflow-wrap: break-word` remains as a last resort; `word-break: keep-all` and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` are never used on Burmese.

### Named Rules
**The Two Ramps Rule.** Burmese never inherits the English sizes, line heights, or tracking. Every new type role ships with its `:lang(my)` pair.

**The Space Is The Break Rule.** Burmese copy is written with spaces at phrase boundaries, and those are the only places a line may break.

**The Measured Fallback Rule.** Every web face has a local fallback sized to it, so a font swap does not reflow the page.

## Layout

One centered column (max 82rem) with fluid gutters (clamp 1rem to 2.5rem). Home sections are padded clamp(4rem, 9vw, 7.5rem) vertically and separated by a single top hairline. Within a section, the heading block sits in a max-width-3xl column, then content opens below at mt-10 to mt-12. Two-column splits appear at 64rem (the hero at 0.92fr / 1.08fr, workflow at 0.8fr / 1.5fr, Myanmar and batch sections near 1:1); ledgers split at 48rem, tool rows and videos at 56rem. Breakpoints in use: 40rem, 48rem, 56rem, 64rem.

Content is organized by rules, not boxes: ledgers and ruled lists open with a 1.5px Toner Ink rule and separate rows with 1px hairlines (dashed for ledger items). Fact rows separate items with a trailing 4px square dot so a wrapped line never starts with a separator.

The masthead is sticky: a 3.75rem bar (paper at 92% with a 10px backdrop blur) over a folio strip. Below-fold home sections use `content-visibility: auto` with `contain-intrinsic-size: auto 1200px`, because Myanmar shaping makes their layout costly. Grid children get `min-width: 0` so a long command never widens a column.

**The Folio Address Rule.** Every page carries the folio strip: `LEXO · NN / Section` on the left, version and licence on the right, in mono, with section numbers in Myanmar digits on Burmese pages.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat paper. Depth is a physical metaphor: things are either on the glass (inset, tonal) or a sheet lifted off it (one soft shadow). Platen, commands, and code blocks use a 1px inset rule ring instead of a border. Raised sheets (install ticket, screenshots) take the single sheet shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(20 21 25 / 0.06), 0 12px 32px -12px rgb(20 21 25 / 0.22)`; dark `0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4), 0 16px 40px -16px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): the install ticket, screenshots, the scan-compare stage.
- **Glass ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--rule)`): platen, command line, code blocks, proof slip.
- **Screen edge** (`0 0 0 1px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.28)` plus Sheet): the outline on a product screenshot.
- **Tab lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.12)`): the selected installer tab lifting off its platen track.
- **Grip lift** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)`): the scan lamp's drag grip, sitting above the screenshot.
- **Lamp glow** (`0 0 0 1px rgb(239 138 31 / 0.35), 0 0 22px 5px rgb(239 138 31 / 0.38)`): the scan lamp only. The one thing that glows.

### Named Rules
**The One Glow Rule.** Only the scan lamp emits light. No other glow, gradient halo, or colored shadow.

## Shapes

Squared, nearly sharp corners in three steps: 2px for hairline objects (tags, inline code, sheets, tab chips), 3px for keys and slips (buttons, commands, ticket, screenshots), 4px for the platen bed. Nothing is pill-shaped or generously rounded. Lines are 1px hairlines and 1.5px ink rules. The install ticket has a torn tear-off top edge (a repeating radial-gradient scallop in the paper color), like a job slip from a copy-shop counter. List bullets are squares; tick lists use a 0.6rem by 2px dash.

**The Registration Mark Rule.** Every piece of real product imagery sits on a platen with four L-shaped corner marks (14px arms, 1.5px stroke, Ink 3 at 70%, inset 6px), echoing the brackets in the logo. Imagery is never floated bare on the page.

## Components

### Buttons
Squared like machine keys, pressed like one.
- **Shape:** squared (3px), min-height 2.75rem, padding 0.6rem 1.15rem, weight 620 at wdth 104.
- **Ink (primary):** Toner Ink fill with paper text. Hover (fine pointers only) mixes 14% scan orange into the ink.
- **Line (secondary):** transparent with a 1px Strong Rule outline; hover darkens the outline to ink.
- **Press:** every button, copy button, and header tool scales to 0.96-0.97 on `:active` over 140ms (cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)).
- **Focus:** 2px Scan Ink outline at 3px offset.
- **On the lid band:** the closing open-source band always uses the dark theme's values (ground #121315, ink #eeeff2, #c3c5cb, #9b9ea6; #0c0d0f when the whole site is dark). Its buttons invert the pair: a paper-filled key (#eeeff2 on #121315, white on hover) and an outlined key (1px #5a5d64, outline turns paper on hover).

### Copy Button
- Raised Sheet fill, 1px Strong Rule outline, 3px, 2.25rem tall, xs step at 560. Copy and check icons crossfade in place (opacity, scale, blur); "copied" turns text Scan Ink with an orange outline. On narrow screens it collapses to a 2rem square icon.

### Command Line
- A platen strip with a glass ring: Ink 3 `$` prompt (not selectable), the command in mono at the ui step, a copy button flush right. Commands scroll sideways, never wrap.

### Install Ticket (signature)
- A Raised Sheet (3px, rule ring plus Sheet shadow) with a torn top edge. Header row holds a folio and a segmented tab track (platen fill, 2px padding) of mono tabs `uv` / `pipx` / `pip`; the selected tab lifts to Raised Sheet with a tiny 1px shadow. The choice persists across tickets and visits.

### Platen and Shot
- Platen: glass fill, 4px, fluid padding, inset rule ring, registration marks. Shot: the image or video inside, 3px, Screen Ground fill, screen edge plus Sheet shadow. Captions sit below at the caption step in Ink 3, max 44rem.

### Scan Compare (signature)
- The real window before and after OCR stacked on the platen. The after image is revealed by `clip-path` above a 3px orange lamp with an orange grip. On load the lamp sweeps once from the top to 62% (2.1s, cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1), 350ms delay), then rests. It is draggable (pointer and keyboard via a hidden vertical range input) with a 44px invisible hit zone.

### Workflow (signature)
- On wide screens, numbered steps on the left drive a sticky bed on the right. Pages crossfade (360ms) as a short lamp pass runs (620ms). The current step's mono number turns Scan Ink; inactive steps dim by color to Ink 3, never by opacity. Proof slips show `del` struck in proof red beside `ins` in proof red; output sheets show the exported file unwrapped, a whole number of lines, fading into the platen.

### Tags
- Mono label step, uppercase at +0.05em, 1px Strong Rule outline, 2px, Ink 2. Burmese tags drop to the Myanmar face, no transform.

### Navigation
- **Masthead:** logo plus `LEXO` wordmark (800, wdth 118), primary links at the sm step, 540, in Ink 2; the current page goes to ink with a 2px orange bar on the masthead's bottom edge. Header tools (language, theme, GitHub) are 2.5rem squared icon keys with a platen hover fill.
- **Mobile (below 64rem):** a menu key opens a full-width panel of md-step rows divided by hairlines; the current row has a 3px inset orange left bar. A full-width ink Download button closes the panel.
- **Docs sidebar:** a hairline left rail; links take a 2px left border, orange when current.
- **Footer:** paper ground, hairline top, a brand column and three link columns headed by folios.

### Prose
- Ink 2 at 1.0625rem, 44rem measure, 1.1em block rhythm. Links underline in orange at 1.5px; inline code on platen chips; code blocks on glass with a ring; tables as hairline rows; blockquotes with a 1px orange left rule; square bullets with Ink 3 markers.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** lay every screenshot and video on a platen with registration marks.
- **Do** spend orange only on the scan, the current/active state, and the primary path; use Scan Ink when orange must carry text or a focus ring.
- **Do** write a `:lang(my)` pair for every new type role: line-height 1.7-1.95, letter-spacing 0, no uppercase, its own size.
- **Do** separate Burmese phrases with spaces or ZWSP in copy, and leave the post-build nowrap pass to make those the only break points.
- **Do** give every new web font a measured `size-adjust` local fallback.
- **Do** keep buttons squared (3px) with the 0.97 press scale and fine-pointer-only hover.
